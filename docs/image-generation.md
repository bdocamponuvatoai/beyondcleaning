# Image generation

All photography on the site is AI-generated and used as the final site assets, without on-page labels. It does not show Beyond Cleaning staff, premises or completed jobs.

The prompts below are the originals from the ChatGPT build (built-in image generation tool). No image contains a logo, brand mark or text.

`public/og.jpg` is a 1200 × 630 JPEG crop of the hero image, made with `sharp`.

## hero

- File: `assets/images/home.webp`
- Used in: Hero (`components/sections/hero.tsx`) and `public/og.jpg`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium NYC cleaning company homepage hero, 3:2 landscape photograph. An exquisitely clean yet lived-in sunlit Manhattan brownstone living room: tall original sash windows on the right, soft ivory linen sofa, sculptural walnut coffee table with a ceramic vase and simple leafy branch, warm pale oak floor, plaster walls, subtle architectural molding and a glimpse of red brick New York buildings outside. Interior editorial photography for an upscale home services brand. Gentle morning sunlight, soft natural shadows, believable material textures, elegant restrained styling, sophisticated warm white and muted sage palette. Wide real architectural lens, straight verticals, inviting eye-level view. It should feel like the reward of professional cleaning, not a luxury real estate render. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark, no glossy CGI.
